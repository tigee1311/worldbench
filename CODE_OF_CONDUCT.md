# Code of Conduct

WorldBench follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1.

## Our Standards

Use welcoming and respectful language, accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best for the project, and show empathy toward other contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, public or private abuse, publishing private information without permission, or conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to `writetoayushadi@gmail.com`. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project productive and safe.

This document is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
